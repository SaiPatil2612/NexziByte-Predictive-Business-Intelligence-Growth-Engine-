============================================================
PURPOSE
============================================================

Reusable pipeline for transforming authorized raw B2B lead
data into clean, validated, enriched, segmented and
marketing-ready intelligence.

============================================================
KEY PRINCIPLE
============================================================

Observed information and inferred opportunities are kept
separate.

Observed information represents evidence present in the
source data.

Inferred opportunity represents a potential NexziByte service
or business opportunity derived from available evidence.

The workflow does not intentionally fabricate missing
persona, technology, product launch, partnership or business
signals.

============================================================
MARKETING READINESS
============================================================

READY
    Usable company identity and sufficient evidence for
    targeted marketing.

NEEDS VERIFICATION
    Potential prospect requiring additional validation.

DO NOT USE
    Invalid or unusable record.

============================================================
LEAD PRIORITY
============================================================

Tier 1 - High Priority
    Prospect Score >= 75

Tier 2 - Priority
    Prospect Score 60–74

Tier 3 - Nurture
    Prospect Score 40–59

Tier 4 - Low Priority
    Prospect Score < 40

DO NOT USE records remain Tier 4.

============================================================
REUSABILITY
============================================================

The workflow is designed to process a new authorized raw
CSV by changing the input file in the Raw Data folder and
rerunning the notebook.

Outputs are saved into the existing project folder structure.

NexziByte_B2B_Lead_Intelligence_REUSABLE_FINAL_MASTER.csv
